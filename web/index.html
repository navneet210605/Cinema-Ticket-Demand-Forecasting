📐 Why WAPE over MAPE?

MAPE divides by individual show sales \(y\), causing infinite errors on low-attendance shows (e.g. 5 tickets). WAPE (Weighted Absolute Percentage Error) normalizes total absolute errors by aggregate ticket volume sold (\(\sum |y - \hat{y}| / \sum y\)), directly measuring overall cinema revenue and seat capacity error.

⏳ Why Chronological Split?

Box office ticket demand is non-stationary and exhibits temporal hype decay. Random cross-validation leaks future knowledge into past predictions (lookahead bias). Chronological splitting mirrors actual production deployment where we forecast tomorrow's shows using only historical data.

❄️ Cold-Start Handling Strategy

Brand-new movies on Day 0 have zero prior sales history. The pipeline handles cold-start through genre/language base coefficients, the T-6h advance booking tally, and dynamic imputation substituting 55% of screen capacity for missing historical slot sales.

🛡️ Physical Bounds Clamping

Machine learning models could theoretically predict negative sales or tickets exceeding auditorium size. Our post-processing layer clamps all predictions: \(\text{advance\_bookings} \le \hat{y}_{\text{clamped}} \le \text{screen\_capacity}\).
